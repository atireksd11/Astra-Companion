# CAD BOM summary (Tier 1)

Half Life Week 2 should import **`hardware/bom-cad.csv`**.

Same columns as the PCB file: `Part, Purpose, Qty, Currency, Unit Price, Total Price, Vendor, Link, Notes`

## Subtotals

| Currency | Subtotal |
|----------|----------|
| **USD** | **$18.60** (motors, wheels, brackets, caster, servo, inserts, screws) |
| **CAD** | **$10.00** (PETG pro-rated for ~300 g of prints) |

Half Life adds every `Total Price` → about **$28.60** mixed (under the **~$30** Tier 1 cap).

## Form hints

- **Tax:** small mechanical order; **$0–2 USD** on the form is often enough if you pay real HST at checkout.
- **Shipping:** not in the cap — bundle Amazon/AliExpress where you can.
- **Screenshots:** one cart per store you actually use; filament can be a single Amazon line for the pro-rated amount you enter.
